## The desktop app is not signed

Your system shows a warning at first start.

- **Windows:** On the SmartScreen window, click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS:** Right-click the app and select **Open**. If macOS still stops it, go to **System Settings › Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**.
- **Linux:** Make the AppImage executable: `chmod +x Functor_0.1.2_amd64.AppImage`.

## Install the Chrome extension

Unzip `functor-extension-v0.1.2.zip`. Open `chrome://extensions`, turn on **Developer mode**, click **Load unpacked**, and select the folder.
